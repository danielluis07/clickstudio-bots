# Personal-data incident runbook

Implements [#69](https://github.com/danielluis07/clickstudio-bots/issues/69), part of [#37](https://github.com/danielluis07/clickstudio-bots/issues/37).

**Draft — pending legal review.** Brazilian counsel must validate the final wording and obligations before go-live; Daniel records that approval.

**Privacy contact:** Daniel Assunção, encarregado, [bombdaniel@hotmail.com](mailto:bombdaniel@hotmail.com), as published at `/privacidade` and in [the shared contact](../modules/legal/public-site.ts). Daniel coordinates response and records the platform administrator responsible for technical work when an incident is reported.

For Tenant content, ClickStudio is the operator and the Tenant is the controller. For ClickStudio's own Member, authentication, billing and visitor data, ClickStudio is the controller; Daniel coordinates its notification duties below.

| Step | Owner | Deadline | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assess | Technical lead (designated platform administrator), with Daniel | Immediately on detection | Open a restricted incident record. Establish affected systems, Tenants, data categories and approximate number of data subjects; assess confidentiality, integrity and availability. Record detection, confirmation and each controller's awareness timestamps with timezone, evidence and unknowns. |
| Contain | Technical lead | Immediately; continue until contained | Preserve necessary evidence before changes. Isolate affected access paths, pause affected Bot replies/jobs and revoke or rotate compromised credentials as appropriate. Engage affected vendors; record actions and impact without copying unrelated Tenant data. |
| Notify affected Tenants | Daniel | Without unjustified delay; **within 24 elapsed hours of confirmation**, including weekends | Contact each affected Tenant's owner through a verified contact channel, in pt-BR. Give known facts: nature, timeline, affected data/subjects, risks, containment, recommended precautions, privacy contact and next update time. Record delivery and follow up if unacknowledged. Send available facts by the deadline and supplement as findings change; keep each Tenant's information separate. |
| Support controller notifications | Tenant's controller/authorized representative; Daniel supplies evidence and technical support | Start immediately; **within three business days of the controller learning that personal data were affected** when relevant risk or harm requires notification | Help assess risk and document the decision, including reasons for not notifying. The controller notifies **both ANPD and affected data subjects**. Support ANPD filing through its official incident channel and clear pt-BR notices to data subjects. Incomplete facts must not delay required initial notification; track supplements under ANPD guidance. The 24-hour Tenant notice does not replace or restart this clock. |
| Recover and close | Technical lead; Daniel approves closure | Verify before resuming affected processing; close after response actions are documented | Correct the cause, verify Tenant isolation and credential safety, then resume and monitor. Give affected Tenants a final update. Record remaining corrective actions with owners and due dates. |
| Retain record | Daniel | Open immediately; update throughout response; retain **at least five years from the record's creation** | Keep an access-restricted, encrypted record outside Git and routine log/cleanup retention, including incidents not notified to ANPD/data subjects. Preserve assessment, timeline, evidence references, containment, notification decisions, copies/delivery receipts and follow-up actions; provide the Tenant its record for controller obligations. Review lawful disposal after retention, subject to legal holds. |

Basis: [published DPA commitments](../components/public-site/terms-of-use.tsx), [legal publication notes](legal-publication.md), and [ANPD incident guidance](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/comunicado-de-incidente-de-seguranca-cis) (Resolution CD/ANPD 15/2024, arts. 6, 9 and 10; checked 2026-10-23). Counsel validates applicable exceptions before launch; this runbook uses the issue's three-business-day response target.
