# Brazilian competitors and their pricing

Research date: 2026-10-19. Decision context: [Brazilian competitors and their pricing](https://github.com/danielluis07/clickstudio-bots/issues/20).

## Scope and evidence

This is a public-offer comparison for ClickStudio Bots: a Tenant connects messaging Channels, a Bot responds, and Members can perform Handoff. It covers 16 vendors/projects sold into Brazil, including infrastructure substitutes and vertical software. It is not a market-share study, product test, procurement quote, or claim that every listed product is equivalent.

Sources below are first-party pages accessed on 2026-10-19. Prices are their displayed amounts, without currency conversion. “Unknown” means the inspected public evidence does not establish the answer; it does not mean a feature is absent. Marketing capability claims remain vendor claims. A monthly equivalent on an annual contract is not a cancel-any-month subscription. Dynamic toggles and surviving legacy pages make some prices provisional.

## What this establishes

The defensible planning conclusion is that a generic “AI replies on WhatsApp for small Brazilian businesses” offer already has direct competition. The profiles below establish a spectrum from developer APIs and managed open-source software to sales automation and vertically integrated scheduling/orders. Enterprise starting prices alone would substantially overstate the available pricing room.

The evidence does **not** establish an underserved industry, willingness to switch, acceptable error rate, customer acquisition cost, or a sustainable ClickStudio price. Those require customer evidence and the existing separate cost/policy decisions. In particular, a price gap between two vendors is not evidence of unmet demand.

## Public offers

### Blip (formerly Take Blip): platform and Blip Go

**Platform:** quote-led Enterprise; Plus/Super comparison exposes 30/50 Members and 2,000/5,000 monthly DAUs, with R$1.40/R$1.25 additional DAUs and R$150/R$100 additional Members. DAU means a Contact on a Channel per calendar day, including unanswered outbound messages. WhatsApp is official BSP; Instagram is supported. Builder, Desk/Handoff, Copilot and AI are advertised; separate AI allowance and Meta markup are unknown. Consultative or partner implementation onboarding is available. Base monthly prices are not exposed in the inspected current page; do not reuse older Lite prices. [Platform plans](https://www.blip.ai/planos/).

**Go:** R$389/month for small businesses, with AI sales responses, Kanban, unlimited organic Conversations and 100 outbound sends/month; extras R$0.69/send. Official Meta Coexistence preserves Business App usage. No native Instagram DM; the page explicitly directs multichannel/complex operations elsewhere. Fifteen-day trial; cancellation anytime; connection uses Business App, QR and WABA selection. This official QR flow is a useful counterexample to classifying every QR connection as unofficial. The public offer does not decompose the R$0.69 into Meta cost and margin or disclose an AI credit ceiling. [Blip Go](https://www.blip.ai/blip-go/).

### Zenvia Customer Cloud

Software list prices/month: Starter R$0 (1 Member, 100 Interactionz), Specialist R$600 (10, 500), Expert R$1,800 (30, 2,000), Professional R$3,900 (50, 5,000); Enterprise generative AI is quote-only. Channel packages are additional, starting R$100/month. Setup: Starter WhatsApp/RCS and Specialist R$649; Expert R$1,999; Professional R$3,999. A 20% software promotion is explicitly limited to new accounts closed by 2026-10-31; never treat it as permanent pricing. Instagram/Messenger do not consume channel-package credit but can consume Interactionz. WhatsApp requires CNPJ-linked activation. The page separates software usage from channel credit: its R$100 example permits 182 WhatsApp sends, not 182 universal Meta-rate messages. AI credit consumption, exact category rates/markup and onboarding deliverables require a quote. Audience: teams managing attraction through support with automation/governance. [Current pricing and billing explanation](https://zenvia.com/precos/).

### Huggy

Current page renders Starter R$0, Intermediário from R$579/month and Custom from R$989/month, one Member each; extra Members R$69.90/R$119.90/R$189.90 monthly. The page has monthly/annual toggles and advertises 20% annual savings, so confirm the selected cadence before procurement. Starter/Intermediário/Custom show 5/20/unlimited flows; paid plans offer workflows/API and extended history. Official WhatsApp providers and Instagram are supported; WhatsApp messages are charged by quantity/category and Starter has restrictions. Optional implementation is R$1,250. AI entitlement and separate AI charges are not established by this pricing page. Audience: teams organizing and automating multichannel service. [Current plans](https://www.huggy.io/pt-br/pricing).

The still-indexed [checkout](https://checkout.huggy.io/) displays a different Básico/Intermediário/Avançado lineup and R$239/R$729/R$1,239 prices. Treat it as conflicting/possibly legacy evidence, not a second current tariff.

### Octadesk

One R$2,499/month includes 3,000 DAUs; Flow R$4,399 includes 5,500; Nexus is quote-only with 9,000. All show unlimited Members, one official WhatsApp API and centralized channels. Woz autonomous responses, Copilot, external tasks and audio transcription are included. Helpdesk is an additional R$130/R$120/R$100 per Member/month by tier. Mandatory one-time onboarding consultancy is quote-only. Its DAU is a rolling 24-hour period triggered by Contact response/initiation, materially different from Blip's calendar-day meter. Meta-template charges, overage and AI fair-use details require contractual confirmation. Audience: structured service operations. [Current pricing](https://www.octadesk.com/precos).

The older [2025 plans page](https://www.octadesk.com/planos-2025) confirms Instagram Direct and official WhatsApp, but advertises different product names/prices; it is channel evidence, not the current price benchmark.

### Leadster

Leadster WhatsApp is advertised from R$497/month for 300 Contacts, with contact-package billing rather than per-message billing, natural-language sales qualification, CRM/calendar integration, follow-up and a dedicated implementation manager through official Meta API. This is a sales-focused Bot competitor; native Instagram availability, overage, AI ceilings and treatment of Meta charges inside the package are unknown. “No per-message billing” does not by itself prove every possible template/Meta cost is absorbed. [Vendor's own offer description, published 2026-08-04](https://leadster.com.br/blog/agente-de-ia-para-whatsapp/).

Do not substitute the website chatbot's separately advertised entry price for the WhatsApp product. The [website pricing page](https://leadster.com.br/preco/) did not expose an unambiguous numeric WhatsApp price in the retrieved text. The article's assertions about changes to Meta policy are outside this report's verified facts; use Meta's own documentation in the separate pricing decision.

### Kommo

Current comparison lists Basic US$20, Advanced US$30, Pro US$45 per Member/month; Enterprise quote-only. Contracts are 6/9/12/24 months, minimum six; installment payments are not monthly cancellation. BRL payment exists, but no verified BRL tariff was captured. Monthly AI credits per seat are 750/1,250/2,250; extra credits cost extra. Basic includes one WhatsApp and Instagram Channel; Advanced three each; Pro unlimited. Advanced has up to three autonomous AI responders; Pro up to 50 plus booking/voice capabilities. Official WhatsApp and Instagram messaging are explicitly listed. External paid integrations can add costs. Meta billing amount/route and implementation fees are unknown. Audience: sales teams needing CRM/funnels alongside messaging. [Pricing, limits and contract FAQ](https://www.kommo.com/br/precos/compare-planos/).

### Chatwoot Cloud (global benchmark available in Brazilian reais)

The localized page displays Startups R$119 and Business R$249 per Member/month **billed annually**. Hacker is free for two Members and 500 monthly Conversations, but live-chat only. Paid tiers advertise all channels and unlimited Conversations subject to conditions, with 300/500 monthly Captain AI credits; extra credits R$129/1,000. Captain includes autonomous answers, drafting, summaries and knowledge-base assistance. Cloud data is hosted in the US. These are first-party localized BRL prices, not a researcher currency conversion. WhatsApp transport setup, Meta billing and onboarding scope need separate confirmation; the pricing page alone does not identify a specific connector. [Localized Cloud pricing](https://www.chatwoot.com/pt-br/pricing).

### ChatWoot BR (independent Brazilian managed service)

Public display: Empreendedor R$56/Member/month (approximately eight Members, three inboxes, one WhatsApp); Profissional R$414/month (10 Members, 15 inboxes, five WhatsApps); Empresarial R$664/month (20 Members, 10 WhatsApps). Monthly/annual toggle exists; annual charges the whole year with a 10-for-12 offer, so verify displayed cadence. Extra WhatsApp R$57. Profissional adds Instagram/Facebook and one-hour onboarding; Empresarial two-plus-hour onboarding. Default connection is WhatsApp Web QR; official Cloud API is optional. AI Tasks are included; autonomous Eva is gradually released in one/three inboxes on higher tiers. AI quotas and official-channel Meta pass-through are unknown. The footer explicitly disclaims affiliation with Chatwoot Inc. [Offer and connection distinction](https://chatwoot.app.br/precos).

### HypeHost Chatwoot VPS

Infrastructure alternative advertised from R$89/month, scalable 6–96 GB RAM, Brazilian datacenter and free initial Chatwoot installation/configuration, including domain/SSL and channels. This is VPS hosting, not proof of a turnkey autonomous Bot subscription. No per-Member/conversation entitlement or bundled model allowance is established. WhatsApp and Instagram transport, ongoing application administration, backups/SLA and Meta billing need confirmation; installing Chatwoot does not establish an official connection. Audience: technically supported businesses/integrators wanting self-hosted inboxes and control of infrastructure. [First-party VPS offer](https://hypehost.com.br/vps-chatwoot).

### Z-API

Ultimate R$99.99 per instance/month; Partner shows R$89.99–R$54.99 by volume. One instance corresponds to the developer connection rather than a full team inbox. Vendor advertises unlimited messages, webhooks, files up to 100 MB, existing personal/business numbers, active phone requirement and two-day trial. It explicitly contrasts its fixed monthly fee with official API per-message charges: classify this advertised path as an unofficial WhatsApp alternative, not Meta Cloud API. Instagram and a packaged autonomous Bot are not established. Developers must supply the inbox, AI, workflow and implementation; total solution cost is consequently unknown. [Z-API offer and FAQ](https://www.z-api.io/).

### Evolution API

Open-source developer substitute, with no verified managed SaaS tariff in the inspected repository. Its README explicitly distinguishes Baileys/WhatsApp Web from official WhatsApp Cloud API; deployments must be classified by the chosen connection, not by the Evolution brand. It integrates Chatwoot, Typebot, Dify and OpenAI, including transcription. These integrations are not an included model-token allowance. Infrastructure, maintenance, integration labor and external model bills remain deployment costs; the official path may incur Meta messaging charges. Instagram capability and managed onboarding are not established here. [First-party repository and connection types](https://github.com/evolution-foundation/evolution-api).

## How to use this evidence for decisions

### Compare the whole bill and meter

Use this analytical procurement formula, not a claimed universal vendor billing rule:

`Tenant bill = software + Members + Channels + metered usage + AI extras + messaging/provider charges + onboarding amortization + paid integrations`

Normalize a candidate offer against the same actual Tenant workload. Record inbound Contacts, active days, outbound templates, Members, Channels and Bot activity separately. DAUs, rolling Conversations, unique Contacts, Interactionz, orders, message sends and AI credits are different units; dividing headline price by one of them does not produce comparable cost per resolved Conversation.

Do not assume that official API means literal at-cost Meta pass-through. The inspected offers demonstrate bundled sends, prepaid channel credit, contact packages and undisclosed treatment. Likewise, an unofficial provider's flat transport fee says nothing about the cost of the application/LLM or its operational reliability. A QR screen alone cannot classify a connection: inspect the actual transport and WABA flow.

### Niche interpretation: hypotheses to validate

| Candidate niche | Evidence already present | What remains a hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small clinics | iClinic sells appointment messaging; scheduling software already owns workflow data | Clinics may value an affordable Bot that handles FAQs and Handoff without replacing their system; willingness to pay and integration access are untested |
| Independent real-estate teams | Jetimob already joins inventory, CRM and WhatsApp qualification | A narrower offer for teams outside that ecosystem may work; property-data freshness, switching costs and demand are untested |
| Beauty businesses | Trinks already performs conversational scheduling and Handoff | Independent-system compatibility or easier onboarding may differentiate; lack of competition cannot be claimed |
| Restaurants | Anota AI automates reception/order routing; Goomer competes at low menu/order prices | A generic FAQ Bot may be weaker than order integration; an unmet subsegment has not been identified |
| Small multichannel service teams | Low-price managed inboxes and official WhatsApp AI offers exist, with different Instagram coverage | Reliable WhatsApp plus Instagram, simple onboarding and clear billing may matter together, but their buying priority is untested |

The report supports asking the existing target-niche decision to choose a specific workflow and reachable customer population. It does not justify choosing one solely from desk research. The existing pricing decision should choose an intelligible unit only after workload and cost evidence; no new standalone research ticket is necessary from these findings alone.

## Evidence gaps and quote checklist

For finalists, obtain a dated written quote specifying: currency and tax; minimum term; selected cadence; exact Channel/Member allowances; metered-unit definition and reset; overage; autonomous AI entitlement and fair use; model-credit charges; actual WhatsApp transport; Instagram DM availability; Meta billing owner, markup and prepaid-credit expiry; setup and migration scope; cancellation; and who owns the WABA/number and exported Conversation history.

This research did not create trials, attach a business number, buy plans, inspect private contracts, or test response quality. Consequently “advertised” and “verified in use” remain distinct. Quotes and product trials may overturn marketing-page assumptions. No report claim relies on competitors' comparison tables, Reddit pricing anecdotes, converted USD estimates or expired Jetimob promotions. Several pages are undated; access date is the observation date, not a guarantee of contract validity.
